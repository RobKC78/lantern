# Insights and AI research

Research now runs inside the app. See [AI-SERVICE.md](AI-SERVICE.md) for the current workflow, privacy boundaries, setup and limitations. The former download-and-share research workflow has been replaced.

Local insights include grouped log patterns, scan comparisons and an optional saved working baseline. Baselines omit raw logs and network addresses. Service-state repair backups are not full-device backups, and automatic undo is not supported. Media discovery identifies advertised capabilities but does not cast content.
